    Glossaries

    One glossary per project. A meeting can use several, or none: pick them in the meeting's Glossary tab, when you re-run it, or in a profile for new recordings. Stored as text files in .glossaries inside your transcript folder.

    Commas or new lines. "#" starts a note, ">" a line of context. Keep it short: only the first 20 or so terms help, a long list gets "heard" where nobody said it.

    No glossaries yet. Make one per project with its names, products and jargon.

    Profiles

    A profile is a set of models, language, glossaries and recording options you pick with one click. Whatever you change while one is active is remembered by it.

    Models

    Speech models for each job. The time is how long the final pass takes for one hour of meeting, learned from your own meetings where possible.

    Find more on Hugging Face

    Search for newer or language-specific Whisper models. Ready models (CTranslate2) download as they are; Transformers models are converted on download, which needs more time and disk. Only your search words are sent to Hugging Face.
